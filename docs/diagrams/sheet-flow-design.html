Key Decisions
  • Actuals in EUR, not FTE — accountant provides EUR; converting back to FTE adds noise and no value.
  • Separate Actuals sheet — Plan stays purely about planning. No source-of-truth confusion.
  • Personnel ceiling only — 25% indirect is a separate grant budget line. Ceiling comparison is personnel-to-personnel.
  • No reporting periods — monthly granularity in Actuals allows ad-hoc slicing when needed.
  • Global params in A–B only — prevents auto-fit from widening month columns.
  • Per-WP ceiling in config area — next to Active Months. Keeps related WP properties together.
Out of Scope
  • Multi-partner consortium view
  • Non-personnel cost tracking
  • Reporting period configuration
  • Grant amendment tracking
  • Per-person actual time logging
